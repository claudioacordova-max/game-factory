## Prompt utilizado

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: concept reference sheet for an existing small browser 3D card RPG, not a final game asset. Create a single landscape sheet split into four clean views without text or labels, attainable with low-poly Three.js primitive geometry. Same elevated isometric camera from +X/+Z with open roof and cutaway foreground walls, human scale 14 by 12 metre rooms. Panel 1: Gothic funerary crypt threshold, slate stone, slender arches and bronze trims, warm torches and teal gate, two distinct entrances, walkable open paths. Panel 2: colder catacombs with mortuary wall niches and two sarcophagi; then ceremonial guardian chamber suggested along back wall. Panel 3: ancient crypt invaded by rats, broken masonry, dirt floor motifs, burrows as dark recesses in far walls, earth ochre olive materials, nest along outside rear edge, keep navigable center. Panel 4: clear simplified character lineup on neutral background: slim adolescent boy in long black coat, broad brim black hat, dark side-swept hair, boots; tall thin spear and shield sentry; mysterious hooded funeral custodian with staff; broad armored crypt guardian; small real quadruped rat and dominant quadruped rat matriarch with crown-like bone crest. Dark gothic fantasy stylized 3D, not anime or photorealism; readable forms under moderate ambient light, muted stone/bronze/teal palette with earthy rat wing, no cinematic darkness, no interface, no cards, no new lore, no text, no massive architecture. Reference only; actual game rules and positions remain unchanged.
